---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview the user about decisions that affect the plan or design. Walk the design tree in dependency order. Give your recommendation with each question.

Ask one question at a time and wait for its answer before moving to a dependent decision. Stop when the remaining choices can be resolved from the user's direction and the available evidence.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase or trying a cheap prototype, do that instead.
