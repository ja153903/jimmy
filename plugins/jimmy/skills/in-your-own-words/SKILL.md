# In your own words

Restate what you think the user wants to achieve and why it matters. Distinguish the goal from a proposed solution or suspected cause. Keep it brief enough for the user to correct a misunderstanding. If the request also asks you to do the work, continue after the restatement unless the user explicitly asks you to stop there.
